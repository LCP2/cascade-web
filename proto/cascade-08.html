How Cascade works

The average person watches ~50 movies a year; an enthusiast up to 300. We're bombarded by choice and noise — knowing how to spend your time on the best movies for you is hard.

🎬 Cascade Agents go and find movies for you — focused on Cinema or Streaming. Have as many as you like; they keep watching and surface new films over time.
🔔 Set them to list movies and notify you when films you'd love are announced, hit cinemas, are available to rent, or land on your streaming services.
🧠 Smart analysis learns what you really watch, and shows which streaming services are worth your money right now.

Less than one rental — $3.99/month. First month free, cancel any time.
